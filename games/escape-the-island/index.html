🏝️ HOW TO PLAY: ESCAPE THE ISLAND

🎯 THE OBJECTIVE

Your team is stranded on an uncharted island. To survive and escape before the 35-minute timer expires, answer English questions, collect required survival items, make smart tactical choices, and launch your escape craft!

👥 TEAM TURNS (45–75 SECONDS)

Teams take turns in a fixed order (Tigers → Lions → Eagles → Sharks...). On your turn, your team discusses the English challenge and gives an answer. Your teacher approves the answer with [✅ CORRECT] or [❌ INCORRECT].

🎒 ESCAPE RECIPES (COLLECT 3–4 ITEMS)

  • 🛶 Ocean Escape Boat: Wood 🪵 + Rope 🪢 + Fuel ⛽ + Tool 🔧
  • 📡 Radio SOS Rescue: Radio 📻 + Battery 🔋 + Tool 🔧
  • 🚁 Helicopter Extraction: Map 🗺️ + Battery 🔋 + Fire 🔥

❤️ ENERGY SYSTEM

Each team starts with 3 Energy Hearts (❤️❤️❤️). Danger events or wrong answers may cost 1 ❤️. If you reach 0 ❤️, you take a quick rest or solve an energy riddle to recover!

⏱️ 35-MINUTE PACING GUARANTEE

The game automatically tracks classroom lesson time. At 28 minutes, island danger escalates to help you gather final missing items. At 33 minutes, all teams enter the final escape window!
